Lean Six Sigma manufacturing optimisation

Precision shaft machining · DMAIC · Independent synthetic case study

All measurements and operating data are simulated. Improvements are scenario results, not verified industrial outcomes.

DMAIC methodology

Define: diameter 25 ± 0.05 mm; defect rate below 2%, OEE above 80%, Cpk ≥1.33.

Measure: 1,000 measurements, 20 independent production shifts. Validate gauge R&R in a real deployment.

Analyse: oversized shafts dominate baseline defects. Candidate causes: insert wear, offsets, setup and coolant.

Improve: model centred offsets, standardised setup, tool-life controls and preventive maintenance.

Control: hourly subgroups of five, shift OEE, weekly Pareto and monthly capability review.
